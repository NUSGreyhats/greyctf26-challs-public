# Name

Watchdog KOTH

# Description

Challenge yourself in evading the dogs. Bring your `.wdog` file onstage and take the least clock cycles you can!

Conditions
1. You have to print the entire flag + '\n' via UART
2. You only can bring up a `.wdog` file, no circuitpython files or any other conditions

The author has one solution of about `0x163` clock cycles, try to match/beat him (it is definitely possible in theory)!

Ignore the flag submission, your score for this challenge will depend on how fast your solution is relative to other teams. This will be issued via an award to your team
